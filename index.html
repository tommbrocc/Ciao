    General tips

    About these results. Scores come from a model trained on the SCUT-FBP5500 research dataset: 2,750 photos of men (2,000 East Asian, 750 white) each rated by 60 volunteers in China. The model predicts those ratings with a correlation of about 0.77 — good, but far from perfect — and it reflects those raters' taste and those photos. Men of other ethnicities are under-represented, so treat results with extra caution if that applies to you. Lighting, lens, head angle and expression all affect the numbers. None of this measures your worth; confidence, grooming, style and personality matter more in real life. Use it as a guide, not a verdict.
